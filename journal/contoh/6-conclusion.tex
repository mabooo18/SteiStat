\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
This work presented STEI-ZStat, a US\$25.36, 63.1 $\times$ 45.0~mm AD5941 potentiostat validated for EIS from 0.2~Hz to 200~kHz. Against a PalmSens4 reference, all fitted parameters satisfied the predefined acceptance criteria and every cross-instrument 95\% confidence interval remained within them, supporting practical equivalence under the tested passive conditions. Three assembled boards reproduced these parameters in supplementary measurements.

This work introduced a four-stage validation procedure: reactance-sign screening, resistive plateau checks, CNLS parameter recovery, and equivalence testing. The first three require no reference instrument, and the procedure is applicable to other low-cost impedance measurement systems.

Future work will extend validation to redox couples, functionalized electrodes, wider impedance ranges, and independently built complete instruments, and will improve high-frequency phase performance and $R_{CAL}$ accuracy. The voltammetric methods implemented in firmware will also be validated, and Kramers--Kronig consistency analysis investigated as an alternative data-validity check.
