\section{Bill of materials}
\label{sec:bom}

Table~\ref{tab:bom} gives the itemized Bill of Materials, generated from
the current PCB layout (\texttt{Production/bom-1.csv},
Section~\ref{sec:design-files}) and cross-checked against the schematic
component values cited throughout this paper (e.g. \texttt{RCAL1}=
\SI{200}{\ohm}, \texttt{R3}=\SI{560}{\ohm}). Unit prices are single-unit
list prices from LCSC (component supplier) except the XIAO RP2040 module,
priced from Seeed Studio's own store; all accessed September 2026.
Component-only total is \SI{31.07}{} USD per unit; this excludes PCB
fabrication and SMT assembly labor.
